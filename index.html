Research

Preprints

  • The Euler characteristic of Spherical Bestvina-Brady Kernels
    Abstract

    We compute the Euler characteristic of Bestvina--Brady kernels $\operatorname{BB}(\Gamma)$ for all Artin groups of spherical type. Our method involves discrete Morse theory to describe the cellular structure of a classifying space for the Bestvina--Brady kernel, through the action of $\operatorname{BB}(\Gamma)$ on non-integer level-sets of the universal cover of the Salvetti complex. We extend the method of computation of $\chi (\operatorname{BB} (\Gamma))$ to Artin groups that satisfy the $K(\pi,1)$-conjecture and whose nerves are rationally acyclic, and we provide an example by realising the projective plane as the nerve of a Coxeter group.
