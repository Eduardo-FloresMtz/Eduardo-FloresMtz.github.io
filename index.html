Research

My research is in geometric group theory, in particular Artin and Coxeter groups, applications of Bestvina-Brady Morse theory, finiteness properties of groups and $L^2$-Betti numbers.

Preprints

  • The Euler characteristic of Spherical Bestvina-Brady Kernels
    Abstract

    We compute the Euler characteristic of Bestvina--Brady kernels $\operatorname{BB}(\Gamma)$ for all Artin groups of spherical type. Our method involves discrete Morse theory to describe the cellular structure of a classifying space for the Bestvina--Brady kernel, through the action of $\operatorname{BB}(\Gamma)$ on non-integer level-sets of the universal cover of the Salvetti complex. We extend the method of computation of $\chi (\operatorname{BB} (\Gamma))$ to Artin groups that satisfy the $K(\pi,1)$-conjecture and whose nerves are rationally acyclic, and we provide an example by realising the projective plane as the nerve of a Coxeter group.

  • Finiteness properties of Bestvina--Brady kernels for graph products with torsion

    Joint work with Luis Jorge Sánchez Saldaña.

    Abstract

    We study Bestvina--Brady kernels associated to graph products whose vertex groups are either infinite cyclic or finite. Using a $\mathrm{CAT}(0)$-cubical complex constructed by Ruane and Witzel on which the graph product, with defining graph $\Gamma$, acts and an equivariant Morse function, we give sufficient conditions for homological finiteness properties in terms of the topology of the flag complex $\Gamma^\blacktriangle$, the subcomplex $\Gamma^\blacktriangle_\infty$ spanned by the infinite vertices, and the ascending and descending links of certain vertices. We also prove a partial converse, showing that, under additional vanishing hypotheses on the homology in the next degree, non-vanishing homology of these complexes obstructs higher finiteness properties of the Bestvina--Brady kernel. The main new features are the presence of finite stabilizers and the resulting additional link conditions, which are expressed inside the flag complex of $\Gamma$. When the finite vertex groups are abelian, we characterize when the kernel has finitely many conjugacy classes of finite subgroups, describe the normalizers of its finite subgroups as Bestvina--Brady kernels of smaller graph products, and obtain a criterion for the kernel to be of Bredon type $\underline{\fp}_n$.
